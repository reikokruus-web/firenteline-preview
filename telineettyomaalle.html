Scaffolding for the worksite

Reliable scaffolding services for construction and renovation.

We offer safe and high-quality scaffolding for the renovation and construction of detached houses, row houses, and small apartment buildings. Our service includes scaffold rental as well as professional installation and dismantling.

Our services

  • Scaffold rental: High-quality and inspected scaffolding for various sites.
  • Scaffold installation and dismantling: Our experienced team ensures safe assembly and dismantling of scaffolds.
  • Fast delivery and flexible scheduling: We deliver scaffolds on the agreed schedule and tailor the rental period to your needs.

Why choose us?

Professional service: Over ten years of experience in scaffolding contracting.
Competitive prices: We offer quality service cost-effectively.
Safety first: We follow strict occupational safety regulations in all our activities.
Local operator: We operate in the Uusimaa region, offering fast response times.
