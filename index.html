Who we are

We are Firen Teline

We serve industrial customers in scaffolding contracting and provide skilled labor for industrial insulation projects. We are familiar with the operating practices of demanding industrial environments and understand the importance of safety, quality, and adhering to schedules. The foundation of our long-term customer relationships lies in responsibility, flexibility, and reliability.
